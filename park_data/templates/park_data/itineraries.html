CUSTOM ITINERARIES AND EXTENSIONS:

In addition to the standard itineraries most commonly requested, you can tailor an itinerary to meet your specific needs. African Stem Safaris can provide arrangements at any of the national parks and reserves or in most of the private ranches/reserves in Kenya and Tanzania. You can select from any locations in Kenya or Tanzania and we can provide all arrangements. It is also possible to select these other locations as optional extension trips for any passengers wishing to extend their stay. Information on some of the more popular alternative locations is listed below.

Creating A Custom Itinerary:

If you are familiar with East Africa, then you need only let us know what you would like in the way of an itinerary. We will return a cost per passenger to you as quickly as possible after knowing how many you are in the group. If you are not familiar with East Africa, then simply return the postcard included in this brochure/ website and we will send you information on other locations, which include detailed descriptions of what your clients can expect to see and experience at each place.

Optional Extension Trip:

While any location in East Africa can be selected as a destination for an optional extension for your group, we highly recommend the coastal area. Nearly all groups entering East Africa from the U.S. enter either through Nairobi or Kilimanjaro Airport outside Arusha, Tanzania. Few enter through Mombasa or Dar-Es Salaam on the coast. Consequently most U.S. Groups miss the extraordinary experience of coastal life. Earnest Hemingway was a frequent visitor to the Kenya coast, saying.... "This cannot be less than natural beauty, the endless sand, the reefs, the lot are completely unmatched in the world." Both Kenya and Tanzania coastal areas offer some of the most unique and exotic travel destinations to be found anywhere.

Coastal extensions

Often groups looking for an extension in East Africa are looking for an experience other than the one, which involves viewing of wildlife. Of particular interest in the Arab, Indian and Swahili cultures which dominate the coastal areas as well as the recreational opportunities. On both the North and South Beaches there seem endless white sand beaches, clear water and protected reefs. In additional to recreational opportunities such as snorkelling, diving, fishing, glass boat tours of the reef, sailing on Arab dhows, windsurfing, bicycling, live bands, stage shows and discos there are numerous cultural experiences.

Lamu: Most popular locations for culture history are; Lamu, a historic mix of Arab and Sawahili cultures where it is possible for your clients to live in a Swahili - Arab culture little changed over the centuries.

Malindi: Offering a most unusual blend of Swahili, Arab and European cultures. Here there are upscale accommodations, shopping and visits to the Gedi Ruins, an ancient swahili city dating back to the 1200s. Beaches here are less populated than those around Mombasa, but still offer special amenities.

Mombasa North or South Beach Areas: A major trading centre with historic sites of Fort Jesus built by the Portuguese in 1590 to defend the port and walking tours of the Old Port of Mombasa, visiting historical homes and government buildings, some dating back to the late 1400s. Shopping for unique gifts is one of the more popular activities.

Zanzibar Island: Our most popular extension. Zanzibar is perhaps most famous as a slave trading port and the most important supplier of spices and cloves. The best base from which to start your cultural tour is Stone Town, the oldest part of the city with a rich Arab and Indian heritage. Walking historical tours and shopping in Stone Town are prime activities.
