KARIBU KENYA-THE BEST DESTINATION IN AFRICA

Welcome to paradise! An unrivalled wealth of wildlife, bird-life, and scenic beauty - through the eyes of Africa Stem Safaris, one of the finest safari companies in Africa.
Kenya is truly a land of contrast with astounding and incredibly dramatic variations of altitude and terrain within its boundaries. With over 40 national parks and reserves, few visitors return home without spotting the big five - Lion, Elephant, Rhino, Leopard and Buffalo.
The Kenya coast too leaves a lasting impression - miles upon miles of unspoilt palm fringed white sandy beaches, stretching for 500 kilometres, make the Kenya coast, the take off point for sunkissed beaches, exciting watersports and for trips into the hinterland.
Experience the "magic" of the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro!
The annual migration of over a million wildebeest, zebra and other plains game with the ferocious predators trailing them to the Mara ecosystem will leave you obsessed until you return to experience this amazing spectacle again and again. Combine with this "mystical" Zanzibar and you will have connoisseured the best of East Africa!
In addition to our modern well-serviced fleet of "Safari Mini vehicles," we have now acquired brand new 4 x 4 land cruisers equipped with long range radio communication to enhance your safari, and keep in touch with our "Safari Operations" base in Nairobi, which is manned 24 hours.
With Twenty-Five years of "hands on" experience in the safari business, I promise you an unforgettable African Adventure at unbeatable value, where you will yearn for that return to paradise!
